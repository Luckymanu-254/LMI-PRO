# LMI PRO Phase 2 Documentation
## Liquidity Engine (v2.0)

## Overview

Phase 2 transforms LMI PRO from a market structure indicator into a liquidity analysis system.

The objective is to identify:

- Liquidity pools
- Major highs and lows
- Liquidity sweeps
- Market events for future AI analysis

## Versions

### v2.0.1 — Liquidity Detection

Features:

- Equal High detection
- Equal Low detection
- Liquidity pool identification

### v2.0.2 — Liquidity Sweep Engine

Features:

- Buy-side liquidity sweep
- Sell-side liquidity sweep
- Price rejection detection

### v2.0.3 — Major Liquidity Filter

Improvements:

- Reduced consolidation noise
- Focused on stronger liquidity areas

### v2.0.4 — Institutional Liquidity Filter

Added:

- Swing strength validation
- Major liquidity filtering

### v2.0.5 — Institutional Liquidity Core

Added:

- Major high liquidity
- Major low liquidity
- Liquidity quality filtering

### v2.0.6 — Market Memory Foundation

Added:

- Event recording
- Liquidity event tracking
- Sweep memory
- Structure memory
- AI preparation layer
