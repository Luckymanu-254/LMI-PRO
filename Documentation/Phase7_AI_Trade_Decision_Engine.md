# Phase 7 – AI Trade Decision Engine

## Status

- Version: LMI PRO v4.2
- Phase: 7
- Status: Completed
- Date: August 5, 2026

---

# Objective

Develop an integrated AI-driven trade decision engine that combines
market structure, institutional concepts, higher-timeframe analysis,
risk management, and a professional dashboard into a single Pine Script
indicator.
