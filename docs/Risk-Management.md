# LMI PRO Risk Management

## Overview

Risk management is the foundation of LMI PRO.

The system prioritizes capital protection before profit generation.

A good analysis without proper risk control is incomplete.


# Risk Philosophy

LMI PRO follows:

- Protect capital first.
- Avoid unnecessary exposure.
- Trade only high-quality opportunities.
- Maintain disciplined risk.


# Risk Evaluation Engine

The system evaluates:

## 1. Risk Percentage

Determines the amount of capital exposed on each opportunity.

Factors:

- Account size
- Risk tolerance
- Market volatility


## 2. Stop Loss Analysis

The system evaluates logical stop placement using:

- Market structure
- Swing highs and lows
- Liquidity areas
- Volatility


## 3. Take Profit Planning

Targets are based on:

- Previous market structure
- Liquidity zones
- Support and resistance
- Risk-to-reward ratio


# Risk Reward Model

Preferred minimum:

1:2 Risk Reward


Example:

Risk:
50 points

Potential reward:
100+ points


# Position Management

The system considers:

- Entry quality
- Market conditions
- Volatility
- Trade invalidation points


# Professional Rules

LMI PRO avoids:

- Overtrading
- Revenge trading
- Low-quality setups
- Trading without confirmation


# Goal

The objective is not to predict every market move.

The objective is to identify high-probability opportunities while controlling risk.
