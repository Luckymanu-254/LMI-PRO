# LMI PRO Trading Logic

## Overview

LMI PRO analyzes markets using a structured process inspired by professional institutional trading methods.

The system does not rely on one indicator. It combines multiple factors to produce a higher-quality market analysis.

---

# Market Analysis Process

## 1. Market Condition Analysis

The system identifies:

- Trending markets
- Ranging markets
- High volatility conditions
- Low volatility conditions

---

## 2. Market Structure Analysis

The system detects:

- Higher High (HH)
- Higher Low (HL)
- Lower High (LH)
- Lower Low (LL)

Used to determine market direction and structure.

---

## 3. Liquidity Analysis

The system identifies:

- Equal Highs
- Equal Lows
- Liquidity pools
- Liquidity sweeps
- Stop-hunt areas

---

## 4. Institutional Analysis

The system studies:

- Order Blocks
- Fair Value Gaps
- Supply and Demand zones
- Institutional reaction areas

---

## 5. Confirmation Engine

The system confirms analysis using:

- Volume
- Divergence
- Momentum
- Price reaction

---

## 6. Risk Evaluation

Before any trading idea, LMI PRO evaluates:

- Entry location
- Stop loss placement
- Take profit area
- Risk-to-reward ratio
- Market volatility

---

# Trading Philosophy

LMI PRO follows these principles:

1. Quality over quantity.
2. Risk management before profit.
3. Multiple confirmations before decisions.
4. Clean charts with minimal noise.
5. Analysis before execution.
