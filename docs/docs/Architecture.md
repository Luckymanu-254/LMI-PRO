# LMI PRO Architecture

## System Overview

LUCKYMANU MARKET INTELLIGENCE PRO (LMI PRO) is a modular market analysis system designed to analyze financial markets using institutional trading concepts, technical analysis, and risk management.

## Core Architecture

Market Data

↓

Market Structure Engine

↓

Liquidity Engine

↓

Institutional Analysis Engine

↓

Confirmation Engine

↓

Risk Management Engine

↓

Professional Dashboard


# Core Modules

## 1. Market Structure Engine

Purpose:

Identify the current market structure.

Functions:

- Swing High detection
- Swing Low detection
- Higher High (HH)
- Higher Low (HL)
- Lower High (LH)
- Lower Low (LL)
- Break of Structure (BOS)
- Change of Character (CHOCH)


## 2. Liquidity Engine

Purpose:

Identify where market liquidity exists.

Functions:

- Equal Highs
- Equal Lows
- Liquidity pools
- Stop hunt detection
- Liquidity sweeps


## 3. Institutional Analysis Engine

Purpose:

Analyze professional trading areas.

Functions:

- Order Blocks
- Fair Value Gaps
- Supply and Demand zones
- Institutional reaction areas


## 4. Confirmation Engine

Purpose:

Increase analysis quality.

Functions:

- Volume analysis
- Divergence detection
- Momentum analysis


## 5. Risk Management Engine

Purpose:

Protect trading capital.

Functions:

- Risk percentage
- Stop loss calculation
- Take profit planning
- Risk reward evaluation


## Development Philosophy

LMI PRO prioritizes:

- Clean charts
- High-quality setups
- Multiple confirmations
- Risk-first decisions
- Explainable analysis
