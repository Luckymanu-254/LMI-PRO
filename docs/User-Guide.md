# LMI PRO User Guide

## Overview

LMI PRO is a professional market analysis system designed to help traders understand market conditions using structured analysis.

The indicator combines:

- Market structure
- Liquidity analysis
- Institutional concepts
- Volume
- Divergence
- Risk management


# Installation

## TradingView

1. Open TradingView.
2. Open Pine Editor.
3. Add the LMI PRO script.
4. Save the indicator.
5. Add it to your chart.


# Recommended Workflow

## Step 1 — Select Market

Choose the market you want to analyze.

Examples:

- Volatility 75 Index
- Forex pairs
- Gold
- Crypto
- Stocks


## Step 2 — Analyze Higher Timeframe

Start with:

- 4 Hour
- 1 Hour

Determine:

- Market direction
- Major structure
- Important levels


## Step 3 — Check Market Structure

Look for:

- Higher Highs
- Higher Lows
- Lower Highs
- Lower Lows
- Break of Structure
- Change of Character


## Step 4 — Confirm Opportunity

Check:

- Liquidity zones
- Institutional areas
- Volume confirmation
- Divergence confirmation


## Step 5 — Manage Risk

Before entering:

Check:

- Stop loss location
- Target area
- Risk reward ratio
- Market conditions


# Chart Philosophy

LMI PRO is designed to keep charts clean.

The system focuses on:

- Important areas only
- High-quality information
- Avoiding unnecessary signals


# User Responsibility

LMI PRO is an analysis tool.

Users should:

- Manage risk properly
- Understand market conditions
- Avoid emotional decisions
- Test strategies before live trading
